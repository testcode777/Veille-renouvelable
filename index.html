Bottom line

This week brings significant regulatory relief for French solar developers, with the environmental assessment threshold raised from 1 to 3 MWc, potentially streamlining project approvals for smaller installations. However, developers are protesting what they characterize as a moratorium on rooftop solar following the government's announcement of its tender calendar. On the funding side, CVE has secured €350 million for development activities, while regional partnerships are emerging, including Groupe Lhotellier and Caisse d'Epargne Normandie's collaboration to accelerate solar deployment in Normandy. Public procurement notices for photovoltaic projects have been published in France, presenting potential bidding opportunities.

Environmental assessment threshold for solar projects raised from 1 to 3 MWc

Decree n° 2026-674 of 27 July 2026, published 28 July 2026, raises the systematic environmental assessment threshold for ground-mounted solar plants from 1 MWc to 3 MWc. Projects between 300 kWc and less than 3 MWc are now subject to case-by-case examination. Projects of 3 MWc or more remain subject to systematic environmental assessment.

Action: Review permitting strategy for projects between 1 and 3 MWc to prepare case-by-case environmental examination files demonstrating minimal environmental impact.
HIGH IMPACT 🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine — Marchés & Politique · 2026-07-29
Developers denounce moratorium on rooftop solar following government tender calendar announcement

On 7 July 2026, Energy Minister Maud Bregeon announced the CRE tender calendar until the 2027 presidential election, including two technology-neutral tenders in October 2026 (500 MW) and potentially January 2027 (volume unannounced). A collective of developers representing over 2.5 GW of installed renewable capacity criticizes this as a de facto moratorium on building-mounted solar, noting that only 10 to 15% of sites subject to APER law solarization obligations are compliant as of 1 July 2026.

Action: Monitor the October 2026 technology-neutral tender and assess eligibility for the 500 MW allocation, while preparing for potential changes to building-mounted solar support mechanisms.
🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine — Marchés & Politique · 2026-07-29
Public procurement notices for photovoltaic projects in France

Multiple public tenders for photovoltaic installations are open in France, including rooftop solar at a hospital in Calvados (deadline 7 August 2026), canopy projects in Corrèze and Nord (deadline 7 August 2026), a ground-mounted plant in Val-d'Oise (deadline 12 August 2026), and rooftop renovation with solar in Calvados (deadline 21 August 2026). Tenders cover feasibility studies, supply, installation, commissioning, and maintenance.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine — Marchés & Politique · 2026-07-28
Groupe Lhotellier and Caisse d'Epargne Normandie partner to accelerate solar energy in Normandy

Groupe Lhotellier and Caisse d'Epargne Normandie have signed a partnership to support energy transition for economic actors in Normandy, facilitating deployment of photovoltaic plants and collective self-consumption loops in business activity zones. Caisse d'Epargne provides financial engineering expertise and access to its Kiwaï crowdfunding platform, which has already supported several photovoltaic projects in Normandy. Meetings for business leaders and local officials have been organized across Normandy.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine — Marchés & Politique · 2026-08-03
CVE secures €350 million financing for development

Marseille-based producer CVE has closed a €350 million financing operation with Macquarie Asset Management to support the development of its solar activities. The company operates a portfolio of over 1 GWc in operation and under construction. The debt will finance development trajectory and optimize the group's financial structure.

🇫🇷  General · Actu-Environnement — Énergie · 2026-07-30
€260 million allocated for adaptation of five ports for floating offshore wind

The French government will invest nearly €260 million in adapting five ports (Cherbourg, Brest, Nantes-Saint-Nazaire, Port-la-Nouvelle, and Marseille-Fos) for floating offshore wind needs under France 2030. Total investments required for deployment are expected to reach nearly one billion euros. France aims for nearly 6 GW of floating offshore wind in service by 2040.

🇪🇺  Solar · pv magazine — Applications · 2026-07-28
Nadara secures €1.2 billion pan-European refinancing for renewable energy assets

Independent power producer Nadara completed a €1.2 billion refinancing covering 47 operating wind and solar plants (1.5 GW total) across seven European countries, including France. The transaction, one of the largest cross-border renewable refinancings in Europe this year, will support repowering, hybrid projects, and battery storage deployment. Fifteen international commercial banks participated, with Crédit Agricole CIB and Santander acting as financial advisors.

Two new agrivoltaic plants commissioned in Côte-d'Or

Akuo has commissioned two agrivoltaic plants in Côte-d'Or department: a 10.9 MW facility at Saint-Martin-de-la-Mer producing 13.8 GWh annually, and a 34 MWc plant at Sombernon on 54 hectares. Both projects include agronomic monitoring throughout their operational life. In late 2025, Akuo raised over €5.1 million from local residents and businesses to finance three agrivoltaic projects including these two.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine — Applications · 2026-07-29
Nîmes inaugurates photovoltaic canopy to support bus fleet electrification

Nîmes Métropole has inaugurated a photovoltaic canopy over its TanGo bus depot, designed to produce approximately 1,300 MWh per year with around 300 MWh self-consumed. The installation supports the city's goal of operating a 100% electric bus fleet by 2030, coinciding with the arrival of new electric articulated buses with 340 to 350 km autonomy.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine — Applications · 2026-07-29
EDP completes 300 kWp photovoltaic plant for automotive supplier Snop

EDP has commissioned a rooftop photovoltaic installation of approximately 0.3 MWp at Snop's industrial site in Étupes, Doubs, comprising over 550 solar panels expected to produce nearly 270 MWh per year. The project is part of a European partnership signed in 2025, with a portfolio reaching approximately 5 MWp capacity and over 6 GWh annual production across Spain, Germany, and France. EDP plans to install around 700 MW of on-site solar solutions across Europe over the next four years.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine — Marchés & Politique · 2026-07-28
Cestas solar plant in Gironde narrowly escapes forest fire damage

The 300 MWc Cestas solar plant near Bordeaux, operated and 40% owned by Neoen, was threatened by forest fires on the weekend of 25–26 July. 34 firefighters from Geneva were deployed to protect the 258-hectare site. The plant was disconnected and evacuated as a precaution and remains offline.
