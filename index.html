Bottom line

Luxembourg has introduced two significant funding developments this week: enhanced heat pump subsidies paired with a new mandatory inspection regime, and an extension of the Klimabonus Wunnen programme that now includes a pre-financing option for heat pumps and renovations. These measures directly impact the financial viability and compliance requirements for heat pump projects in your Luxembourg portfolio. A new Luxembourg law addresses state contributions to a compensation mechanism for 2026, though the specific implications remain unclear from available information. On the technology front, Geo2Watts is developing a borehole battery platform that could store curtailed solar electricity as heat, potentially relevant for integrating your geothermal and solar assets, though this appears to be an early-stage development.

Luxembourg approves enhanced heat pump subsidies and mandatory inspection regime

The Luxembourg parliament approved on 13 July 2026 the restoration of the Klimabonus Wunnen scheme retroactive to 1 January 2026, after its legal basis expired at the end of 2025. An additional subsidy of 2,000 euros will be granted for geothermal and air-water heat pumps installed in existing homes, applicable for equipment ordered before 30 June 2027 and invoiced before end of 2028. The scheme will remain in force until 31 December 2030, with temporary increases from tripartite negotiations valid until 30 June 2027.

Deadline: 2027-06-30
Action: Assess eligibility for the 2,000 euro additional subsidy for geothermal heat pump projects in existing Luxembourg residential buildings and ensure equipment orders are placed before 30 June 2027.

Luxembourg extends Klimabonus Wunnen with pre-financing option for heat pumps and renovations

The revised Klimabonus Wunnen scheme was adopted by parliament on 15 July 2026 and applies retroactively from 1 January 2026. Starting 1 January 2027, participating installers can deduct subsidies directly from invoices for heat pumps and eligible renovation work, then claim reimbursement from the state. The main scheme runs until 31 December 2030, while complementary tripartite measures end earlier.

Action: Register as a participating installer to offer pre-financing of subsidies to customers starting 1 January 2027, reducing upfront costs for heat pump and renovation projects.

Idex commissions 12.1 MW rooftop solar installation with 3 MW battery at ID Logistics Amiens site

Idex inaugurated on 17 July 2026 a rooftop solar installation of 12.1 MW on a 118,403 m² warehouse in Amiens, using 26,000 panels covering 61,000 m². The project includes 1.8 MW of carport solar on two parking lots and a 3 MW battery for grid injection smoothing. Total investment was 15 million euros.
