Small-Scale Solar Market Experiences Severe Contraction in 2025

France's small solar segment (≤9 kW) contracted sharply in 2025, with installations under 3 kW declining 45.4% and the 3–9 kW segment falling 17.6%, following regulatory changes in March 2025 that eliminated full-production sales to EDF and reduced feed-in tariffs from 12 cents to 1 cent per kWh. Equipment prices fell 7.3% for 3 kW systems to €2.15 HT per watt, while labor costs rose 5%.

Action: Monitor regulatory changes affecting feed-in tariffs and autoconsumption incentives; assess impact on project economics for small-scale solar developments.

Agrivoltaics Sector Calls for Revised Criteria in Upcoming CRE Solar Tenders

France Agrivoltaïsme has urged the government to revise selection criteria for upcoming Commission de Régulation de l'Énergie (CRE) solar tenders, arguing that price alone should not determine project selection and that agricultural, climate, and territorial benefits must be weighted. Many agrivoltaic projects are now at financing stage after years of development under the new regulatory framework.

🇫🇷  Both · Actu-Environnement — Énergie · 2026-07-21
CRE Forecasts 2027 Public Energy Service Charges Rise Driven by Renewable Volume Growth

The French energy regulator (CRE) published its initial estimate that public energy service charges (CSPE) will reach 14.2 billion euros in 2027, financed by 10.67 billion euros from the state budget and 3.56 billion euros from energy taxes on non-interconnected zones where electricity production costs exceed mainland levels. The article indicates charges are rising due to increased renewable energy volumes.

Draft Law on Land Tax and Land Mobilization Tax in Luxembourg

Luxembourg has proposed comprehensive amendments to its tax code covering land tax, property valuation, income tax, and urban development regulations. The draft modifies multiple foundational tax laws dating from 1931 to 2022 but does not specify energy-related provisions in the available text.

Construction Group NGE Acquires Stake in Renewable Energy Developer Gaïa Energy Systems

French construction firm NGE has acquired a stake in Gaïa Energy Systems, a Marseille-based renewable developer operating hydroelectric, geothermal, and solar assets across France. The combined entity targets 200 MW of renewable generation capacity by 2030 and has acquired three floating solar projects totaling 13 MW in Occitanie and Nouvelle-Aquitaine.

SNCF Continues Testing Removable Solar Panels on Railway Network

France's SNCF railway operator is continuing pilot tests of removable photovoltaic technology from Swiss startup Bankset Energy Group on its 48,000 km rail network since January 2025. A parallel test with SunWays technology began April 2026 in Switzerland with 18 kWc installed across 100 meters of track, scheduled to run until 2028.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-23
Vegetation Management Protects 11 MWc Solar Plant from Wildfire Damage

A Luxel-operated 11 MWc solar facility in Trévillach, France, sustained minimal damage (approximately 100 panels out of 43,500) during a July 2026 wildfire that burned 5,000 hectares, demonstrating the effectiveness of mandatory vegetation clearance within 50 meters of perimeter fencing. Regulatory compliance with fire prevention standards and rapid grid disconnection by Enedis limited equipment damage.
