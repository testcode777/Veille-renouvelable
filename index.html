Small-scale solar market experiences severe contraction in 2025

Installations under 3 kW declined 45.4% in 2025 compared to 2024, while the 3–9 kW segment fell 17.6%, marking the first major downturn since 2011 after eight years of continuous growth. The collapse was driven by regulatory changes in March 2025 restricting sales to surplus production only, a tariff drop from 12 cents to 1 cent per kWh, and reduced autoconsumption subsidies. Equipment prices fell 7.3% but labor costs rose 5%.

Action: Monitor regulatory developments affecting small-scale solar economics and assess impact on project viability in France.

Agrivoltaics sector urges government to revise criteria for upcoming CRE solar tenders

France Agrivoltaïsme association has called on the government to modify the selection criteria for future Commission de Régulation de l'Énergie (CRE) solar tenders, arguing that price alone should not determine project selection. The association requests greater recognition of agricultural, climate, and territorial benefits, noting that many prefectural-approved projects are now reaching financing stage after years of development.

Action: Track CRE tender criteria updates and assess potential opportunities in agrivoltaic projects if multi-criteria selection is adopted.

Vegetation clearance protects 11 MWc solar plant from major wildfire damage

An 11 MWc solar facility operated by Luxel in Pyrénées-Orientales sustained only minor damage during a major July 2026 wildfire that burned 5,000 hectares and displaced 10,000 residents. Regulatory vegetation clearance within a 50-meter perimeter acted as a firebreak, protecting 43,400 panels; only 100 panels and some cables were damaged. The operator emphasizes compliance with mandatory clearance obligations and rapid recovery procedures.

Action: Ensure all solar sites comply with mandatory vegetation clearance (OLD) requirements and establish fire prevention protocols aligned with departmental fire service recommendations.

CSPE 2027: French Energy Regulator Forecasts Increase Driven by Renewable Energy Volumes

The French Energy Regulation Commission (CRE) published its initial estimate of public energy service charges (CSPE) for 2027, projecting €14.2 billion in total costs. Financing will come from €10.67 billion in state budget allocation and €3.56 billion from energy taxes on non-interconnected zones where electricity production costs exceed mainland levels.

Action: Monitor CRE's final CSPE determination for 2027 to assess impacts on project economics and power purchase agreement terms.

Construction group NGE acquires stake in renewable energy developer Gaïa Energy Systems

French construction company NGE has acquired a stake in Gaïa Energy Systems, a Marseille-based renewable developer operating hydroelectric, geothermal, and solar assets across France. The combined entity now controls five hydroelectric plants, one large ground-mounted solar facility, two rooftop solar portfolios, and multiple deep geothermal projects around Étang de Berre. NGE and Gaïa target 200 MW of renewable generation capacity by 2030, with recent acquisition of three floating solar projects totaling 13 MW in southern France.

Action: Monitor NGE-Gaïa partnership developments for potential collaboration or acquisition opportunities in French solar and geothermal sectors.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-24
SNCF continues testing removable solar technology on railway network

French railway operator SNCF is conducting ongoing trials of removable photovoltaic systems from Swiss startup Bankset Energy Group on its 48,000 km rail network since January 2025. A parallel pilot with SunWays technology began in April 2025 in Switzerland, featuring 48 panels (18 kWc) over 100 meters of track, scheduled to run until 2028. SNCF is also developing solutions via subsidiary AREP for non-operational lines.
