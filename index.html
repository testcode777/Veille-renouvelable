Bottom line

Luxembourg has introduced two significant policy updates this week that may affect project economics in that market. The government has approved enhanced subsidies for heat pumps, though these now come with mandatory inspection requirements that could add compliance costs. Separately, the Klimabonus Wunnen energy renovation subsidy programme has been extended through 2030 and will now offer a pre-financing option, potentially easing cash flow constraints for renovation projects. Both measures apply to Luxembourg only and do not directly impact the French pipeline, though they may influence the relative attractiveness of heat pump integration in Luxembourg-based developments.

Luxembourg approves enhanced subsidies and mandatory inspections for heat pumps

The Luxembourg parliament approved on 13 July 2026 the restoration of the Klimabonus Wunnen scheme retroactive to 1 January 2026, after its legal basis expired at the end of 2025. An additional subsidy of 2,000 euros will be granted for geothermal and air-water heat pumps installed in existing dwellings, with equipment ordered before 30 June 2027 and final invoices issued before the end of 2028. From 1 January 2027, participating contractors may deduct the subsidy directly from the customer invoice and claim reimbursement from the state.

Luxembourg extends Klimabonus Wunnen energy renovation subsidies until 2030 with pre-financing option

The revised Klimabonus Wunnen scheme applies retroactively from 1 January 2026 and will remain in force until 31 December 2030, with temporary tripartite increases ending earlier. From 1 January 2027, installers may deduct subsidies for heat pumps and eligible renovation work directly from invoices and request reimbursement from the state. Pre-financing is already available for photovoltaic installations and associated batteries and will extend to heat pumps and eligible renovation work from 2027.

Action: Prepare to utilize the pre-financing mechanism for photovoltaic and geothermal heat pump projects starting 1 January 2027 to reduce upfront customer costs.
