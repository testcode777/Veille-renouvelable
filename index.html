These funding windows present immediate opportunities for project development in the Luxembourg market. Luxembourg approves enhanced heat pump subsidies and mandatory inspection regime

The Luxembourg parliament approved on 13 July 2026 the reinstatement of the Klimabonus Wunnen scheme retroactive to 1 January 2026, after its legal basis expired at the end of 2025. An additional subsidy of 2,000 euros will be granted for geothermal and air-water heat pumps installed in existing homes, with equipment ordered before 30 June 2027 and final invoices issued before end of 2028. A new mandatory inspection regime for heat pumps after installation and throughout their lifetime was also approved.

Luxembourg extends Klimabonus Wunnen energy renovation subsidies until 2030 with pre-financing option

The revised Klimabonus Wunnen scheme was adopted by parliament on 15 July 2026 and applies retroactively from 1 January 2026. The main scheme will run until 31 December 2030, while temporary tripartite measures end earlier. From 1 January 2027, installers can deduct subsidies directly from invoices for heat pumps and eligible renovation work and claim reimbursement from the state, reducing upfront costs for households.

Action: Prepare to register for the pre-financing mechanism available from 1 January 2027 to offer reduced upfront costs to customers.
HIGH IMPACT 🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-24
Small-scale solar market suffers severe slowdown in 2025

According to Observ'ER, French photovoltaic installations under 3 kWc fell 45.4% in 2025 compared to 2024 (153,250 kW), while the 3–9 kW segment declined 17.6% (from 685,000 to 564,100 kW). Since March 2025, installations between 0 and 9 kWc can no longer sell all production to EDF OA but only surplus; the purchase tariff collapsed from 12 cents to 1 cent per kWh, and the self-consumption premium was sharply reduced.

Action: Review business model for residential solar projects under 9 kWc in light of the new surplus-only sale rule and 1 cent/kWh tariff effective since March 2025.

Agrivoltaic sector calls for revised criteria in upcoming solar tenders

France Agrivoltaïsme urges the government to amend the Commission de régulation de l'énergie's forthcoming tender criteria, arguing that project selection based solely on price ignores agricultural, climate and territorial benefits. The association warns that many agrivoltaic projects authorised by prefectures and awaiting financing are at risk. It calls for clear guidance in the next tender rounds to secure investments and maintain confidence.

French energy regulator forecasts rise in public service energy charges for 2027 driven by renewable energy volumes

The Commission de régulation de l'énergie (CRE) estimates that public service energy charges (CSPE) will reach 14.2 billion euros in 2027. The state budget will cover 10.67 billion euros, while energy taxes will contribute 3.56 billion euros for non-interconnected zones (ZNI). The increase is attributed to growing renewable energy volumes.

🇱🇺  Both · Paperjam · 2026-07-23
Luxembourg's renewable energy share rises but lags EU leaders

Renewables represented just over one fifth of Luxembourg's gross final energy consumption in 2025, up 5.5 percentage points from 14.7% in 2024, according to provisional Eurostat figures published 23 July. The EU average reached 26.2%. Renewable electricity covered 23.3% of Luxembourg's gross electricity consumption, less than half the EU average of 49.9%. Luxembourg must reach 37% renewable energy by 2030 under its national energy and climate plan.
