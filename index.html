HIGH IMPACT 🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-24
Small-scale solar market suffers sharp slowdown in 2025

According to Observ'ER, the French market for photovoltaic installations of 9 kW or less declined sharply in 2025. Installations under 3 kW fell 45.4% compared to 2024 (from 153,250 kW), while the 3–9 kW segment dropped 17.6% (from 685,000 to 564,100 kW). Since March 2025, full feed-in tariffs are no longer available for 0–9 kWc installations; only surplus sales are permitted, with the purchase tariff collapsing from 12 cents to 1 cent per kWh, and self-consumption premiums severely reduced.

Action: Review business model for residential solar in light of the new surplus-only regime and 1 cent/kWh tariff effective since March 2025.

Agrivoltaic sector calls for revised criteria in upcoming solar tenders

France Agrivoltaïsme urges the government to revise the criteria for upcoming CRE solar tenders, arguing that selection based solely on price ignores the agricultural, climate and territorial benefits of agrivoltaic projects. Many projects compliant with the new regulatory framework are now reaching financial close and await adapted tender conditions. The association warns that price-only criteria threaten the sector and undermine agricultural interests.

French Energy Regulator Forecasts Public Service Energy Charges to Reach €14.2 Billion in 2027

The French energy regulator (CRE) published its first estimate of public service energy charges (CSPE) for 2027, projecting €14.2 billion in total. The state budget will cover €10.67 billion, with €3.56 billion from energy taxes allocated to non-interconnected zones (ZNI). The increase is driven by higher renewable energy volumes.

Mexens (formerly Technique Solaire) secures €288 million financing

Independent power producer Mexens raised €288 million in senior debt to finance a portfolio of 230 renewable projects in France (approximately 180 MW) and Spain (approximately 64 MW). The financing, arranged with Bpifrance, Crédit Agricole and other banks, includes wind projects alongside solar photovoltaic for the first time. The company targets over 4 GW of installed capacity by 2030.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-24
SNCF continues testing solar panels on railway network

SNCF is testing removable photovoltaic technology from Swiss startup Bankset on a Paris technical site since January 2025, with modules that can be mounted or removed in under two minutes. A separate pilot with SunWays, launched 24 April 2025 at Buttes (Neuchâtel canton), features 48 panels of 380 W (18 kWc total) on 100 metres of active track and will run until 2028. SNCF is evaluating multiple technologies for railway-compatible solar deployment across its 48,000 km network.

🇱🇺  General · Paperjam · 2026-07-23
Luxembourg's renewable energy share rises to 20.2% in 2025

Renewables accounted for just over one-fifth (20.2%) of Luxembourg's gross final energy consumption in 2025, up 5.5 percentage points from 14.7% in 2024, according to provisional Eurostat figures published 23 July. Renewable electricity covered 23.3% of gross electricity consumption, less than half the EU average of 49.9%. The EU-wide 2030 target is 42.5%; Luxembourg's national target under its PNEC is 37%.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-23
Vegetation clearance protects photovoltaic plant from wildfire damage

An 11 MWc solar plant operated by Luxel in Ille-sur-Têt (Pyrénées-Orientales) sustained only minor damage when a wildfire burned 5,000 hectares on 4 July. Only two tables (approximately 100 panels) out of 43,500 were damaged, along with some cables; transformers and inverters remained intact. The operator attributes the limited damage to mandatory vegetation clearance (OLD) within a 50-metre perimeter and to prompt disconnection by Enedis. Half the inverters were back in service by 10 July.
