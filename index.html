HIGH IMPACT 🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-24
Small-scale solar market experiences severe contraction in 2025

Installations under 3 kW declined 45.4% in 2025 to 153,250 kW, while the 3-9 kW segment fell 17.6% to 564,100 kW, marking the first major downturn since 2011 after eight years of continuous growth. The collapse was driven by regulatory changes in March 2025 restricting sales to surplus production only, the purchase tariff falling from 12 cents to 1 cent per kWh, and reduced autoconsumption subsidies. Average installation prices for 3 kW systems fell 7.3% to 2.15 €/W including labor but excluding storage.

Action: Monitor regulatory changes affecting small-scale solar economics and assess impact on project pipeline and pricing strategy.

Agrivoltaics sector urges government to revise criteria for upcoming solar tenders

France Agrivoltaïsme association called on the government to modify CRE tender criteria to account for agricultural, climate and territorial benefits beyond price alone. The sector reports numerous projects authorized by prefectures are reaching financing stage after multi-year development processes, with farmers seeking protection against drought and climate impacts through these installations.

Action: Track upcoming CRE tender announcements and assess whether agrivoltaic criteria changes create opportunities for project development.
🇫🇷  Both · Actu-Environnement — Énergie · 2026-07-21
CSPE 2027: CRE Anticipates Increase Driven by Renewable Energy Volumes

The French Energy Regulatory Commission (CRE) published its initial estimate of public energy service charges (CSPE) for 2027 at 14.2 billion euros, to be financed by the state budget at 10.67 billion euros and energy taxes at 3.56 billion euros for non-interconnected zones. The increase is attributed to growing renewable energy volumes.

Action: Monitor CRE's final CSPE determination for 2027 to assess impact on project financing costs and power purchase agreement economics.

Construction group NGE acquires stake in renewable energy developer Gaïa Energy Systems

French construction company NGE acquired a stake in Gaïa Energy Systems, a Marseille-based renewable developer operating hydroelectric, geothermal and solar assets across France. The combined entity targets 200 MW of renewable generation capacity by 2030 and holds a portfolio including five hydroelectric plants, one large ground-mounted solar facility, two rooftop solar portfolios, and multiple deep geothermal projects around Étang de Berre. Gaïa simultaneously acquired three floating solar projects totaling 13 MW in Occitanie and Nouvelle-Aquitaine.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-24
SNCF continues testing removable solar technology on railway network

French railway operator SNCF is testing removable photovoltaic systems from Swiss startup Bankset Energy Group on its 48,000 km rail network since January 2025. A parallel pilot with SunWays technology launched 24 April 2025 comprises 48 panels of 380 W across 100 meters of track for 18 kWc capacity, scheduled to run until 2028. SNCF is evaluating multiple technologies to assess feasibility of railway-compatible solar compatible with network operations.

🇫🇷  Solar · pv magazine France · 2026-07-23
Vegetation clearance protected 11 MWc solar plant from major wildfire damage

An 11 MWc solar facility operated by Luxel near Trévillach in Pyrénées-Orientales sustained only minor damage when a major wildfire burned 5,000 hectares on 4 July 2026. Only two of 43,500 panels were damaged; transformers and inverters remained intact. Luxel attributed the protection to mandatory vegetation clearance (OLD) within a 50-meter perimeter around the site, which acted as a firebreak.
